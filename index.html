Well Rundaily

Startups that run well. Paced by funding and buzz, curated daily.

Pace = momentum: open roles (all teams), how recent the last round was, traction (ARR, customers, headcount) and red flags. Checked weekly.

How the score is calculated

Score = Hiring + Funding + Traction − Red flags (never below 0)

Hiring
Open roles, all teams: 30+ = 2 · 10–29 = 1.5 · 3–9 = 1 · 1–2 = 0.5 · none = 0
Funding
Last VC round: within 6 mo = 1.5 · 6–12 mo = 1 · 12–24 mo = 0.5 · older = 0. +0.5 for a $100M+ round in the last 24 mo. Capped at 2. Debt, PE, secondaries and tenders don't count.
Traction
Strong ARR, customer or headcount growth = 1.5 · some = 0.75 · none = 0
Red flags
−1 each for significant issues in the last 24 mo: layoffs or shrinking headcount (>5% YoY), down rounds, major lawsuits, exec exodus. Every flag links its public sources.

Zones: 4.75+ Sprint · 4.25–4.74 Race · 3.5–4.24 Tempo · 1.5–3.49 Steady · under 1.5 Warm-up. Early companies (seed, or a Series A of $15M or less) also sit in Warm-up until they show strong traction. Cool-down = under 1.5 and last round over 24 mo old or shrinking.

    Distance = staying power: Pace shows how fast a company is running now. Distance shows whether it can keep going for years. It's a separate label and doesn't change the score or the order.

    • 42kmMarathonBuilt to last. A real edge, public proof it's working, and little that could knock it out.
    • 21kmHalfThe edge is forming. Some proof, but not locked in yet.
    • 10km10KFast, but easy to catch. Rivals can copy it, or it depends on someone else.
    • —UnprovenToo early to tell. No lasting edge visible yet.
    How Distance is calculated

    1. The edge: what gets stronger with every customer? Each company gets one or two:

    Hard to leave
    The product runs core work, so leaving is painful (payroll, books, billing)
    Grows with users
    Each new user makes it better for everyone else (marketplaces)
    Cheaper at size
    More volume makes it cheaper to run (data centers, clinics)
    Learns from use
    Usage makes the product smarter (AI trained on real work)

    Speed, brand, funding and famous founders help a company get going, but they don't count as an edge.

    2. Proof (+): public evidence that the edge works: net revenue retention over 110% or low churn, a second product customers actually use, raising prices without losing customers, and no reliance on a few giant accounts.

    3. Exposure (−): platform risk (it runs on a rival's system), clone risk (big AI labs or incumbents can ship the same thing) and burn risk (growth only lasts while new money keeps coming).

    Rating starts with market position: Marathon (42 km) = category leader with a lasting edge and proof at scale · Half (21 km) = a leader or strong #2–3 with broad adoption · 10K = behind bigger rivals, easy to copy, or exposed · Unproven = too early to tell. Missing data counts as neutral, not as a strike. Every rating links its public sources; open a company to see them.
